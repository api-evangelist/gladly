Most customer service script libraries are designed to safeguard the organization rather than serve the customer, following a formulaic pattern of acknowledging frustration, validating concerns, citing policy constraints, and presenting a solution. Gladly argues this approach leaves customers feeling processed rather than genuinely helped, and offers phrases and scripts that build loyalty instead.
