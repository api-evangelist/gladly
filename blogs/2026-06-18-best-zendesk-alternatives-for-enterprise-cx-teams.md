This comparison argues enterprise teams typically leave Zendesk due to fundamental architectural limitations rather than a need for cheaper options, pointing to ticket-based foundations that fragment customer interactions across channels, layered AI rather than integrated intelligence, and hidden costs from add-on pricing. It compares Zendesk alternatives for enterprise CX teams on architecture, AI, and predictable pricing.
