WISMO ("where is my order") refers to customer inquiries about order status, a high-volume issue that can consume 60-70% of support agent time. The article explains that reducing these tickets requires proactive shipping updates, self-service tracking, and AI integration with order management systems, rather than simply answering each query individually.
